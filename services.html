Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our immigration services

How do I know which visa is right for me?
The best visa depends on your background, goals, and timeline. During your free evaluation, we'll assess your qualifications and recommend the optimal pathway. Factors include your nationality, education, work experience, achievements, and whether you need temporary status or permanent residence.
What is premium processing and should I use it?
Premium processing is an optional service that guarantees USCIS will process your petition within 15 business days for an additional fee ($2,805 as of 2024). We recommend it for most clients who need faster timelines or certainty. It's available for O-1, H-1B, L-1, and EB-1 petitions.
Can I apply for multiple visa types simultaneously?
Yes, in many cases you can pursue multiple pathways at once. For example, you might apply for an O-1A visa for immediate work authorization while also filing an EB-1A green card petition for permanent residence. We often recommend this dual-track strategy for clients with strong profiles.
Do I need a job offer for all visa types?
No. While H-1B and L-1 visas require employer sponsorship, the O-1A can be filed through an agent, and both EB-1A and EB-2 NIW green cards allow self-petitioning without a specific job offer. This makes these options popular with founders and entrepreneurs.
What happens if my petition is denied?
With our 98% approval rate, denials are rare. However, if a petition is denied, you typically have options: filing an appeal, submitting a motion to reopen with additional evidence, or refiling with a stronger case. We work with you to determine the best path forward.
